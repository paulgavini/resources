Help

Drag a lesson to another lesson's blue left-edge drop zone to place it before that lesson. Drag the dotted line beneath a week label to resize that row, or double-click it to reset. Press Ctrl+Z (Windows) or Cmd+Z (Mac) to restore the most recently deleted lesson. Changes save automatically in this browser. In Chrome or Edge, choose Save once to also auto-save to a JSON file.
