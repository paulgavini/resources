Host 1 : Today we are learning about the water cycle.
Host 2 : What is the water cycle?

Host 1 : The water cycle is the continuous movement of water around Earth.
Host 2 : Where does it begin?

Host 1 : It starts with evaporation, when the Sun heats water in oceans, rivers, and lakes.
Host 2 : What happens after evaporation?

Host 1 : The water vapor rises into the atmosphere and cools down to form clouds.
Host 2 : Is that process called condensation?

Host 1 : Yes, exactly. Condensation forms the clouds we see in the sky.
Host 2 : Then how does the water return to Earth?

Host 1 : It falls as precipitation, such as rain, snow, or hail.
Host 2 : And where does that water go?

Host 1 : Some water soaks into the ground, while some flows into rivers and oceans.
Host 2 : So the cycle keeps repeating?

Host 1 : That's correct. The water cycle is an ongoing process that helps sustain life on Earth.
