Physics guide ↗
1 · Set up

Prepare the echo tube

  1. Close one end of a 1–2 metre tube and measure its internal length.
  2. Place the device microphone as close as possible to the open end.
  3. Make one sharp finger snap or click beside the microphone and tube opening during the recording.

Tip: Keep speaker volume muted to avoid feedback. A quiet room and a hard tube end produce a clearer reflection.
