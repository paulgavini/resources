DUCKWEED PHOTO TOOL

Help and practice photos

Use this guide to estimate the visible duckweed area and the percentage of a dish covered in a photo. The image is processed in your browser and is not saved by the app.

Measure a photo

  1. Choose Upload photo to select an image from your device, or choose Use camera to take a top-down photo. Keep the full inside rim of the dish visible.
  2. Check the dotted circle around the dish. Drag its edge to move it and use the round handle to resize it until it follows the inside rim.
  3. Enter the dish's measured inside diameter in millimetres. This is needed to calculate area in cm²; coverage percentage can be shown without it.
  4. Inspect the colour mask. Adjust the HSB sliders to include duckweed and exclude background, reflections, and other pixels. Use Auto-suggest to suggest thresholds again from the current photo.
  5. Use the mask toggle to show or hide the highlight. Review the visible area and coverage results, then copy them into your spreadsheet.

Getting useful estimates

Use a matte, even background and repeatable diffuse lighting. Fit the circle and inspect the mask before recording a result. Glare, shadows, reflections, overlapping fronds, camera angle, and threshold settings can all affect the estimate.

The tool measures visible surface area only. Photo colour is not a direct measurement of photosynthesis, and a high coverage percentage does not show how much biomass is present once the dish is nearly covered.
